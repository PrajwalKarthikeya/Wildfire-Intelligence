## 👩‍💻 Technical Terms

Phase 12 focused purely on **Developer Experience (DX)** and **Technical Communication**.

*   **Action**: Rewrote the root `README.md`.
*   **Architecture Diagram**: Leveraged Mermaid.js to render a native, interactive flowchart directly in GitHub's Markdown engine. This visualizes the decoupling of the pipeline, persistence layer, API, and frontend.
*   **Methodology Documentation**: Documented our iterative ML journey (from the Logistic Regression baseline to the HistGradientBoosting champion model). Engineering managers don't just want to see code; they want to see *how you think*. By documenting the failure of the linear baseline, you prove that you understand model selection and bias/variance tradeoffs.
